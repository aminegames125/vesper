## Start with the guides

- [Kodi guide](docs/KODI.md): install, first run, recording, watching, [troubleshooting](docs/KODI.md#troubleshooting).
- [Server guide](docs/SERVER.md): options, Docker Compose, reverse proxy, lite and full mode.
- [README FAQ](README.md#faq-and-troubleshooting): the most common problems in a few lines.
- The [website](https://amineos.dev/vesper/).

## Still stuck?

- **Something is broken:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/aminegames125/vesper/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). The more of these you include, the faster it gets fixed: Kodi version, device, server mode (lite or full), add-on version and the relevant lines of `kodi.log`. Remove passwords and provider addresses from anything you paste.
- **You have an idea:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/aminegames125/vesper/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **A security problem:** do not open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What the project cannot help with

Vesper ships no channels and no playlists, and the maintainers cannot help you find IPTV providers or fix a provider's own streams. Problems with Kodi itself or with other Kodi add-ons belong with their own projects.

This is a small project maintained in spare time, so replies can take a few days.
